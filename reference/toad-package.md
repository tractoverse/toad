# toad: Tractography Open Atlas Data

Provides easy access to reference white matter tractography atlases for
benchmarking and anomaly detection studies of brain microstructure. Two
datasets are available: (1) the HCP1065 population-averaged tractography
atlas with 87 white matter bundles derived from 1,065 Human Connectome
Project (HCP) Young Adult subjects (Yeh 2022, distributed under CC BY-SA
4.0); and (2) per-subject TractSeg white matter bundle segmentations of
72 tracts in 105 HCP Young Adult subjects (Wasserthal et al. 2018,
distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0 – non-commercial use only). Both datasets
originate from the HCP and are subject to the applicable HCP data-use
terms. The data are hosted as releases in the package's GitHub
repository and are downloaded on demand as R 'fiber' bundle objects; no
data files are bundled with the package itself. The package code is
distributed under the MIT license; the underlying atlas data remain
under their respective Creative Commons licenses.
